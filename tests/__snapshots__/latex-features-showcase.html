9Images

Figures use the figure environment. The \caption{} becomes the caption, and each figure gets an automatic number you can point at once you give it a \label{}: \Cref{} prints the word and the number — this one is Figure 1 — and \ref{} the number alone, exactly as LaTeX does.

As shown in Fig. 1, images are automatically numbered and captioned.
